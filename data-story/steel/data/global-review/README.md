# Project-level checks on net steelmaking capacity

[review.json](review.json) records this review of 57 plants from the September 2026 Global Iron and Steel Tracker. The review covers all 30 quantified construction sites in China, all 13 in India, and 14 in Austria, Canada, Czechia, Germany, Italy, Oman, Romania, Spain, Sweden and the United Kingdom. Other research in the dashboard may cover additional countries.

Each key is a GEM plant ID. `netMt` measures the documented eligible increment in millions of metric tonnes of annual crude-steel capacity. A positive amount is capped at the construction capacity in the snapshot. Zero means an identified replacement, a project revoked before the snapshot, or construction already completed. The decision and reason distinguish these outcomes. A null amount means the evidence does not resolve the status, process or net change. A missing key means this file does not review that site.

Use the capacity of the predecessor works immediately before conversion when a new furnace replaces production. The temporary gap between the old furnace shutting and the replacement starting does not create new capacity. For a relocation or permit transfer, subtract the explicitly assigned retirements at all linked works. This can leave the destination with more capacity and the host country with no increase. A permit ledger establishes a change in authorized capacity. It does not, by itself, date the physical closure of each donor furnace. The Zhenshi record retains uncertainty because some transferred capacity rights came from steelworks that had closed years earlier.

The source list distinguishes project approvals, issuer disclosures, completion inspections and government notices reproduced by industry publications. Later reports are used where they identify the timing of a September milestone or provide evidence about an earlier project. The six positive entries in this file total 19.75 Mt/yr. This subset cannot establish the total expansion of global steelmaking. The remaining 51 entries include 25 with zero eligible tonnes and 26 with unresolved quantities.
